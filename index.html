Before you apply

Eligibility requirements

Requirements depend on the product and lender. Applicants may be asked to provide:

  • Valid contact details and identity information.
  • Residential and employment or business details.
  • Income information and supporting documentation.
  • Consent for the checks described in the application.

Never share a mobile-money PIN, one-time passcode (OTP), banking password or card security code as part of a loan application.

FAQs

Common questions

How do I find out which loan products are available?

Call us on 0740 613560 to ask about current products, eligibility and documented terms.

How much will I repay?

The amount depends on the product, principal, fees and repayment schedule. Review the full written cost and schedule before accepting any offer.

How long does a decision take?

Review times can vary based on the application and any checks or supporting documents required. A specific timeline is not guaranteed.

Will I ever need to share a PIN or OTP?

No. Do not share your mobile-money PIN, OTP, banking password or other account credentials with anyone claiming to process a loan.
